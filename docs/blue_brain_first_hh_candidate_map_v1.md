## 2) First candidate decision

Decision: **the first later-HH candidate is `Basal Ganglia ↔ Cerebellum` as a relation, not a region.**

Why this relation is selected:

- It is the only Prompt-1 surface already classified as `HH theoretically plausible later` on a concrete relation rather than as generic diagnostic tooling.
- It has the best current leverage/risk quotient because the UCF-relevant question is narrow: selection-channel suppression from Basal Ganglia versus timing/correction support from Cerebellum may eventually need an excitability-near diagnostic vocabulary.
- The relation keeps HH relevance tied to a bounded inter-region seam instead of opening a broad region microcircuit or global model platform.
- It is still only a later candidate: no HH implementation, no third model deepening, no runtime authority and no execution authority are opened.

## 4) Minimal HH relevance of the selected relation

For `Basal Ganglia ↔ Cerebellum`, HH relevance means only a possible later diagnostic vocabulary for:

- excitability-near response threshold at the relation seam;
- spike-like event timing and refractory-shape summaries;
- membrane-proxy sensitivity to bounded stimulus/current-like inputs;
- stability or failure diagnostics for timing/correction versus selection suppression.

Explicitly not modeled here:

- no ion-channel simulation;
- no neuron or compartment model;
- no spiking neural network;
- no Cerebellum or Basal Ganglia microcircuit;
- no execution policy, action selection, retry logic or compute scheduling;
- no conversion of diagnostic output into productive contract state.

The relevance is UCF-functional, not biological completeness: it is only interesting if a later prerequisite step can show that abstract contracts and bounded Kuramoto-like timing/synchrony diagnostics cannot explain a specific relation-level selection/timing diagnostic need.

## 5) Separation from abstract and Kuramoto-like modes

- Abstract alone may eventually be insufficient only for this relation if a later evidence fixture demonstrates that selection suppression and timing/correction diagnostics require membrane-near threshold, event timing or refractory summaries.
- Kuramoto-like remains preferable for the two current selective model deepenings because their current value is coupling, synchrony, gating and timing modulation, not membrane dynamics.
- Kuramoto-like may cover a timing/coupling subaspect of `Basal Ganglia ↔ Cerebellum`; it does not by itself prove or require HH.
- HH remains `later-HH/deferred`, not current mode, because no inputs, outputs, fixtures, performance envelope, or contract-safe authority changes are approved in this prompt.
